## Description

Plans change. People need to cancel leave they asked for, and get the days back.

The policy (section 7) already says how: pending ones can be withdrawn,
approved ones need the manager to confirm, and once the leave started you
cannot cancel it yourself.

Managers should see cancellation requests in their list too.

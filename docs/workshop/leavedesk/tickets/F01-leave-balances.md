## Description

People ask HR every day how many days they have left. We need everyone to see
their own leave balance in LeaveDesk, per leave type, for this year.

- Annual leave is 12 days, plus the seniority day for people who have been with
  us long enough (see the policy).
- New people should only get annual leave for the months they work this year.
  Khoa joined in August, so he should not see 12.
- Sick leave: 5 days a year paid by the company.
- Unpaid, wedding, bereavement: I think people should see them too, but they are
  not really a "balance"…
- HR (me) needs to see everybody's balance on one page, otherwise I still have
  to open the Excel file.

Right now nobody has taken any leave in LeaveDesk, so "used" is 0 for everyone.
Later, approved requests will reduce the balance.

Please put the balance on the dashboard, it is the first thing people look for.
